# Playtest report: Undead of Night: Last Round
Date: 2026-11-07. Build: v26.11.8-191 (live, https://undeadofnight.forthexp.com/). Tester: PLAYTESTER (headless Chromium, desktop 1440x900 and iPhone emulation 390x844). Screenshots are in the tester workspace under shots/.

## Scope reached and its limits
Reached: title screen, the 4-step setup stepper (Arcade, Solo, Stay Close, default survivor Night), Level 1 (Silent Ruins) twice, the game-over screen and Play Again.
NOT reached: levels 2 and 3, any horde survived, pickups collected, the van bench.
Why: my only way to drive the game was synthetic DOM events through browser_eval. A scripted bot with no real aiming could not kill a single zombie, so it died to Horde 1 both times. Difficulty-curve verdicts below are therefore weak.

## Bugs and polish, ranked by user cost
1. MEDIUM, unconfirmed on a real device: no on-screen touch controls visible on mobile (iPhone emulation, 390x844).
   Steps: open the site in iPhone emulation, Play, Arcade, Next, Solo, Next, Stay Close, Next, Start. Expected: a visible joystick or fire button. Actual: none in the screenshot, and no DOM element matched touch/joystick/stick class names. The controls may be canvas-drawn or appear only on a real touch, and I did not dispatch touch events. Repeatability: 1 of 1. A real-device check is needed before calling this a bug.
2. LOW-MEDIUM: HUD text clipped at the right edge on a 390 px viewport. The bottom-right block reads "0:00 SILENT RU" and "L: LO" (should be SILENT RUINS and L: LOG). Seen in 4 screenshots (mobile emulation). Fix: wrap or shrink the block, or shorten the level name on narrow screens.
3. LOW-MEDIUM, 1 observation: horde count mismatch at death. During Horde 1 the HUD said "0 of 2 hordes survived". The frame at death read "Game over 1 of 2 hordes survived", but the game-over screen said "Hordes survived 0". Steps: Play Level 1 and die during Horde 1. Expected: the same number in both places. Seen once; not retried.
4. LOW, unverified: the elapsed clock in the bottom-right HUD stayed at "0:00" for the whole pre-horde minute (log entries every ~4 s, 0:00 each time). It may be a per-horde clock by design. If it is a game clock, it is frozen. Ask the dev.
5. LOW, discoverability: Space, F and Ctrl did not fire. Only a held pointer (about 700 ms) fired, 3 rounds per hold. If keyboard-only play is intended, there is no fire key. I did not read HOW TO PLAY, so I cannot say whether this is documented. Short synthetic taps (200 ms, no buttons flag) did nothing, which I put down to my test input, not a game bug.
6. POLISH: ammo readout "12 / 3" next to a weapon-slot total of "15" is easy to misread. The first number is the magazine and the second the reserve, and both are small and grey. Consider labelling or enlarging the reserve.
7. POLISH, difficulty: on Stay Close, Horde 1 is 7 zombies. A player who stood still lost 80 of 100 HP in about 6 s. A moving, non-aiming bot lost all 100 HP within about 27 s of the horde arriving. That is steep for the first horde of Level 1, but a skilled player may handle it. Not a verified problem.

## Checked and fine
- Load: the page returned HTTP 200. Initial JS is 329,944 B, CSS 26,222 B, and the logo image is 116 KB. Other chunks are lazy-loaded and small (each under 17 KB). Google Analytics (181 KB) loads from Google. No failed requests. Console had no errors or warnings across title, setup and Level 1.
- Setup stepper: all four steps advance and back-navigation buttons are present. Style/mode copy renders correctly (Campaign vs Arcade descriptions, Solo/Multiplayer, 4 difficulties, 5 survivors).
- Level 1 intro banner ("LEVEL 1 OF 10 / SILENT RUINS / SURVIVE 2 HORDES, THEN REACH THE VAN") appears and the horde countdown starts at about 2:20.
- Enemy readability: zombies are dark silhouettes with glowing yellow eyes. They read clearly in lit areas and on a dark ground, but those at the screen edge in the dark are hard to see. The player and the burning barrel are clear.
- Fire, reload and movement work with correct input (held keys moved the player, a held pointer fired, R reloaded).
- Game-over screen is clear. Play Again restarts Level 1 cleanly with the intro banner and 100 HP. A restart did not break firing.

## Could not test
Levels 2 and 3; difficulty curve with real play; pickups and loot; audio (no audio tool, the Sound Test page was not opened); Nightly Run; Levels, Records, Options, How to Play pages; co-op by code; real touch input; frame rate under a horde; Safari; refresh or back-button mid-run; double-clicked submits; very long callsigns.

## Suggested next steps for the lead
Have a human, or a headed browser with real mouse input, play levels 1 to 3 on a desktop and a real phone. Check the touch controls first, then audio.
